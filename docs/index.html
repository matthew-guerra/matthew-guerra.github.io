Research

I am a Latino, first-generation college student, and PhD candidate in the Sociology Department at the University of Oregon. My research focuses on understanding how settler colonialism and racial capitalism have interrupted Indigenous communities relationships to more-than-human kin, land, and their own communities and the role it plays in the racialization process.

I previously worked as a research fellow with the Indigenous Sentinels Network for the tribal government of St. Paul Island. This work involved traveling across Alaska to support ecological monitoring efforts by other Alaskan Native communities. I have a B.A. in sociology from California State University, San Bernardino and a MS in sociology from the University of Oregon. I am also part of the FireGeneration Collaborative and Water Climate Trust, where I contribute to community-centered research.
